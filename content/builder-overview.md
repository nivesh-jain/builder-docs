# Builder Overview

Welcome to the Documentation Hub of Thob Studio! Here, you'll find everything you need to know about our cutting-edge 3D Experience Orchestrator—simply called Builder. Whether you're a seasoned 3D artist, a creative designer, or an enthusiastic frontend developer, this documentation is your gateway to mastering the power of Builder and unleashing your creativity in the realm of web-based 3D experiences.

## What Is Thob Studio?

Thob Studio is on a mission to revolutionize how businesses and individuals engage with digital content through immersive technologies. Our flagship product, Builder, is an innovative platform that allows users to craft interactive, web-ready 3D experiences without the need for extensive coding or manual setup. Whether you're building a product configurator, crafting an immersive website, or enhancing user engagement through dynamic visuals, Builder is your all-in-one solution.

## Why Documentation Matters

Navigating new software can be daunting, especially when it comes to complex tools like Builder. That's why we've crafted this documentation with clarity and precision, ensuring that you can hit the ground running without any hassle. Our goal is not only to teach you how to use Builder but also to empower you with the knowledge to troubleshoot common issues and customize your experience according to your unique needs.

## What You'll Find Here

This documentation is structured around key topics designed to guide you through every aspect of working with Builder:

- **Getting Started**: A comprehensive guide to help you set up your account, install the tool, and get a first glimpse of its capabilities.
- **Core Concepts**: Dive deep into the foundational elements that make Builder tick—from components and hierarchy to themes and logic. See [[Attributes Overview]] and [[Components Overview]] for deeper dives into attributes and UI components.
- **Building Experiences**: Step-by-step instructions on how to create specific types of experiences, including product configurators, dynamic showcases, and user engagement tools.
- **Production Readiness**: Tips for scaling your use of Builder in a team environment, from version control to collaborative workflows.
- **Troubleshooting**: A troubleshooting guide that covers common issues you might encounter while using Builder, along with practical solutions.
